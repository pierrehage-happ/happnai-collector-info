happnai-collector

This is the automated collector run by happnai (happn.ai), an events guide for Ottawa. It reads public event listings so people can find things to do locally.

  • It identifies itself as "happnai-collector" and follows each site's robots.txt rules.
  • It makes few requests, spaced out, and checks pages only as often as needed.
  • It collects public event details only (title, date, place, link). No personal data, no logins.

To block it or ask a question, email happnai.ai@gmail.com. We reply within 10 business days and will stop at once.
